A few good questions

Before we
get started.

Which platforms do you manage?

We choose the platforms that fit your audience and available content. Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn can be included in an agreed plan.

How many posts are included?

The number of posts, formats, platforms, and revisions is defined in your proposal. We plan around a sustainable content schedule rather than promising a fixed package here.

Are paid ads included?

Paid advertising is a separate scope unless your proposal explicitly includes it. Ad spend is paid separately from content or management fees.

Do I get to approve the content?

Yes. We agree on a review schedule and publish approved content. Photography, filming, creator content, and inbox coverage are scoped explicitly when needed.
