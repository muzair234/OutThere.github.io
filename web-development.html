A few good questions

Before we
get started.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can keep what works and improve the design, structure, content flow, and mobile experience. We review the existing platform before recommending a rebuild or an update.

Can you build an online store?

Yes. We can scope product pages, collections, variations, cart, checkout, and payment or shipping integrations. The final setup depends on your catalogue, business location, and chosen providers.

Will I be able to update it myself?

We can include an editable content management system. We agree on what you need to update, then provide a walkthrough at handover.

How much does a website cost?

We quote after understanding the number of pages, content, integrations, and functionality. You receive a clear scope and payment schedule before work starts.
